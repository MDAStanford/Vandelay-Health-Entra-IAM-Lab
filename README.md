# Vandelay Health Identity & Access Management Portfolio

Hands-on Identity and Access Management portfolio demonstrating Microsoft Entra ID and Active Directory administration, identity lifecycle management, access governance, RBAC, automation, and security controls through real-world business scenarios.

---

## About Vandelay Health

**Vandelay Health** is a fictional healthcare technology company headquartered in Santa Monica, California and the company behind the **Ninja Sleeper** — an ultra-light, compact and virtually noiseless CPAP system designed for travelers who need to sleep comfortably in flight without disturbing fellow passengers.

The technology behind the Ninja Sleeper began as a **federal government contract project**, developed to provide military personnel in the field with a quiet and highly portable sleep-apnea solution. Vandelay Health later adapted the technology for the commercial market, incorporating as much of the original proprietary intellectual property as possible into the consumer Ninja Sleeper platform.

That history creates real identity and access challenges. Vandelay must protect sensitive product information and intellectual property while supporting employees, executives, contractors, acquired teams, and external partners working across multiple locations.

In this portfolio, I serve as Vandelay Health's **IAM Administrator**, responsible for managing identity and access across the company's Microsoft Entra ID and Active Directory environments.

**Each lab addresses a real-world IAM issue at Vandelay Health**, from employee onboarding and organizational changes to access governance, privileged access, external identities, authentication, and Conditional Access.

Each project follows the IAM process from **business requirement → identity and access design → implementation → validation and governance**.

## IAM Lab Portfolio
### Lab 01 — Entra User Administration
Establishes Vandelay Health's workforce identity environment through user creation, identity attributes, organizational structure, group membership, and administrative account management in Microsoft Entra ID.

### Lab 02 — Conditional Access
Introduces Conditional Access to protect Vandelay Health resources by applying identity-based access controls and authentication requirements while maintaining appropriate employee access.

### Lab 03 — SAML Single Sign-On
Integrates an enterprise application with Microsoft Entra ID using SAML-based Single Sign-On, demonstrating how Vandelay can centrally manage authentication to third-party applications.

### Lab 04 — Application RBAC
Implements role-based access to an enterprise application, demonstrating how Vandelay can control application permissions according to business responsibilities rather than granting uniform access to all users.

### Lab 05 — Joiner & Mover Identity Lifecycle
Addresses organizational growth and employee change by onboarding new employees, processing a promotion, updating identity attributes, and assigning appropriate group-based access as Vandelay expands its Travel department and Toronto Innovation Center.

### Lab 06 — Access Reviews
Implements an access-review process to determine whether users should retain existing access, demonstrating periodic access recertification, reviewer accountability, remediation, and governance evidence.

### Lab 07 — Identity Data Remediation
Examines the identity and entitlement data underlying an access certification, identifies inconsistencies and access exceptions, and remediates the data before it is relied upon for governance decisions.

### Lab 08 — Privileged Identity Management
Addresses the risks associated with privileged administrative access by applying governance controls to elevated roles and reducing reliance on permanently assigned administrative privileges.

### Lab 09 — Dynamic Groups & Identity Attributes
Standardizes workforce identity attributes and uses attribute-based dynamic groups to automate group membership, reducing manual administration and establishing scalable access rules.

### Lab 10 — Active Directory Bulk Onboarding
Simulates Vandelay Health's acquisition of a Toronto design studio and the integration of seven employees into Active Directory using organizational units, standardized identity attributes, security groups, CSV data, and PowerShell-based bulk provisioning.

### Lab 11 — Location-Based Conditional Access
Addresses the security implications of a C-level external consultant working internationally while collaborating with Vandelay teams, using location-aware Conditional Access to balance business access with identity security.

### Lab 12 — External Identity Governance
Extends Vandelay's IAM program to external identities, addressing how contractors, consultants, partners, or other non-employees receive appropriate access without being treated as members of the internal workforce.
